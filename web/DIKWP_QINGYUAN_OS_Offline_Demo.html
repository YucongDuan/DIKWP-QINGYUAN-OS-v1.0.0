Rights, appeal and boundaries

Consequential measures require a named authorized person. This prototype does not delete content, reduce reach, file reports, freeze payments or diagnose individuals.
  • A false-positive safeguard protects criticism, disclosures of wrongdoing, minority views and public-interest material.
  • Temporary measures must expire, undergo review and allow appeal.
  • Unknown effects remain unresolved obligations; observed outcomes must calibrate models and may justify their retirement.
