# Intervention Ladder

## L0 — Preserve and surface

Used for evidenced warnings, criticism, public-interest reporting, and content below action thresholds.

## L1 — Provenance and evidence return

Attach source identity, evidence status, commercial relationship, uncertainty, and update conditions.

## L2 — Context and counterevidence

Show claim scope, alternative explanations, missing tests, and a bounded correction channel.

## L3 — Cognitive friction

Recommend share delay, purchase cooling-off, autoplay-off, session breaks, citation confirmation, and a visible exit.

## L4 — Amplification or monetization circuit break

Recommend removing compulsive ranking boosts, pausing affiliate-derived recommendation revenue, disclosing sponsorship, and auditing refund/outcome claims.

## L5 — User-controlled temporary quarantine

A temporary hold may be proposed only when high stakes, low evidence, and manipulation or vulnerable targeting coincide. The record remains preserved. Expiry and appeal are mandatory.

## L6 — Authorized human review

Domain, policy, legal, medical, financial, safeguarding, or platform review according to actual authority. The runtime does not adjudicate.

## L7 — Urgent safety escalation

Only for declared imminent danger. This is a human safeguarding route, not automated punishment.

Every action records target, reason codes, owner, expiry, reversibility, appeal, and execution authority. The runtime executes none of them.
