# Source and Claim Boundary

The conceptual architecture draws on DIKWP as an open role network: reproducible sameness, consequential difference, task-relative closure, configured value, and executable future boundary. The implementation is an independent engineering prototype.

The system does not claim that all misinformation, manipulation, addiction, ideology, or human vulnerability can be reduced to one mathematical quantity. It also does not claim that its synthetic scenarios establish real-world safety or effectiveness.
