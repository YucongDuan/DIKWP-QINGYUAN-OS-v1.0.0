# QINGYUAN Interoperability Profile 1.0

A conforming assessment package should expose:

1. item and claim identifiers;
2. source and provenance fields;
3. claim evidence and scope;
4. non-aggregated risk/protection dimensions;
5. at least two non-isomorphic world models;
6. claim-level reasons;
7. intervention target, level, owner, expiry, reversibility, and appeal;
8. explicit public-interest and dissent protection;
9. explicit `automatic_external_action_authority = 0` unless a separate, audited execution layer is authorized;
10. outcome and reversal records;
11. typed residuals and reality debt;
12. append-only provenance or equivalent tamper evidence.

A conformance claim must not imply factual correctness, legality, platform compliance, clinical effectiveness, or moral authority.
