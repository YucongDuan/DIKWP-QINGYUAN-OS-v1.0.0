# Limitations and Research Agenda

The prototype uses declared metadata and lexical/design signals. It cannot independently browse, verify citations, infer private intent, determine legality, diagnose a person, or measure actual harm reduction. It may miss coded language and may over-trigger on quotation, dialect, activism, or satire.

Priority research tasks:

- multilingual and cross-cultural validation;
- independent annotation with affected-community participation;
- adversarial testing for censorship and coordinated reporting;
- calibrated source/provenance verification;
- causal measurement of exposure reduction versus displacement;
- user-controlled recommendation and addiction-friction trials;
- economic audits of pseudo-knowledge and affiliate ecosystems;
- appeal fairness and reversal latency;
- long-term support outcomes in offline high-control situations;
- model revision and retirement after measured failure.
