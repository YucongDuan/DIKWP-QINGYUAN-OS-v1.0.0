# Non-Aggregated Risk and Protection Model

The profile contains twelve independent dimensions:

1. evidence deficit;
2. certainty mismatch;
3. provenance deficit;
4. manipulation pressure;
5. addictive design;
6. vulnerable targeting;
7. commercial conflict;
8. pseudo-knowledge packaging;
9. potential harm;
10. autonomy risk;
11. suppression protection;
12. negative-truth preservation.

No dimension can compensate for another. A high public-interest protection value cannot make a medical claim true; a high harm value cannot justify suppressing an evidenced institutional critique. Decisions preserve these conflicts.

Evidence is assessed claim by claim. Opinion and satire are not forced into factual verdicts. Absence of submitted evidence yields `UNSUPPORTED_IN_SUBMITTED_RECORD`, not “proven false.”

Signal lexicons are screening devices only. Quotation, reporting, and satire reduce direct-attribution confidence. Human review remains necessary for high-impact action.
