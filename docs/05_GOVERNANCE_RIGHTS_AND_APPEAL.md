# Governance, Rights, and Appeal

## Required action fields

Every proposed action must specify:

- named owner and legal/organizational authority;
- claim-level reasons;
- exact target: content, ranking, notification, payment, transaction, or support process;
- expiry and review time;
- reversibility or compensation;
- evidence available to the affected party;
- independent appeal route;
- kill/reversal condition.

## Appeals

Appeals may challenge provenance, evidence weighting, quotation/satire context, audience inference, commercial relationship, proportionality, or least-restrictive means. Temporary restrictions expire unless affirmatively renewed with new reasons.

## No blacklist governance

The runtime produces item-level and claim-level records. It does not issue permanent person, group, ideology, culture, religion, political identity, or mental-health risk labels. Repeated patterns may justify additional review, but every event remains independently inspectable.
