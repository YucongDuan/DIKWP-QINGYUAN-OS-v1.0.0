# Threat Model

## Threats addressed

- fabricated or unverifiable high-stakes claims;
- testimonial laundering;
- urgency, scarcity, secrecy, shame, fear, and authority pressure;
- addiction-oriented interface design;
- vulnerable-audience targeting;
- undisclosed sponsorship and affiliate incentives;
- pseudo-knowledge products and outcome claims;
- high-control groups that combine payment, isolation, and identity pressure;
- coordinated amplification and engagement gaming.

## Threats against the system

- ideological capture of the reviewer;
- coordinated false reporting;
- use of fact-checking as political suppression;
- quotation and satire false positives;
- adversarial spelling and euphemisms;
- minority dialect bias;
- source laundering through apparently reputable intermediaries;
- synthetic evidence and forged provenance;
- appeal denial or indefinite temporary restrictions;
- displacement of harmful content to encrypted/offline channels.

## Mitigations

- plural world models;
- claim-level evidence and source lineage;
- dissent/public-interest firewall;
- no aggregate score;
- no automatic external action;
- expiry and appeal;
- tamper-evident ledger;
- outcome monitoring and model retirement;
- external provenance verification in production deployments.
